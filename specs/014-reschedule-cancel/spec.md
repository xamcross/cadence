**Feature Branch**: `014-reschedule-cancel`

**Status**: Draft

> Backlog reference: **F20 — Flow A3: Reschedule & Cancellation** (Tier 2 — Required MVP, P2). Spec refs: product spec §4 Flow A3, §5.1 (FR-5). First item in the post-F13 "Resilience" branch of the delivery sequence. It directly extends the F13 single-stage scheduling flow (F12 rule engine, F10/F11 calendar adapters, F21/F22 email) and the F14 candidate scheduling page; F13's single-use token model, atomic slot reservation, and audit trail were explicitly designed to be extended here.
>
> **OD-1 carried forward (backlog v1.2.0)**: no automatic video-call link generation in the MVP. Interview location/dial-in remains recruiter-provided free text carried onto the re-booked calendar event. **SMS/WhatsApp nudges remain deferred to v1.5** — reschedule/cancel notifications are email-only.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Candidate reschedules without contacting the recruiter (Priority: P1)

A candidate who already booked an interview realizes they have a conflict. From the confirmation email they received, they click "Reschedule," land on a mobile-friendly page (no login), see a fresh set of currently-available, rule-compliant times in their own time zone, and pick a new one. Cadence atomically moves the interview to the new time — cancelling the old calendar events, creating new ones for every participant, and sending updated invites and a fresh confirmation to all parties — without the candidate ever emailing the recruiter. The candidate's existing interview is never lost if the new booking cannot complete.

**Why this priority**: This is the core value of Flow A3 and the most frequent reschedule path — candidate-driven conflicts. It removes the manual back-and-forth that a reschedule normally triggers (the backlog target: a reschedule costs a human under 60 seconds). It is independently demonstrable from a single confirmed booking and a candidate link, and it delivers value on its own even if recruiter-initiated reschedule (Story 2) is not yet built.

**Independent Test**: Starting from a confirmed booking, open the reschedule link, pick a different available slot, and confirm that (a) the old calendar events are cancelled on all attendees' calendars, (b) new events are created at the new time, (c) updated invites and a new confirmation are dispatched, (d) the audit trail records the old slot, new slot, initiator, and timestamp — all without authenticating.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a candidate with a confirmed booking and a valid reschedule link, **When** they open it, **Then** the page loads a freshly-computed set of compliant, genuinely-free slots in the candidate's local time zone, excluding the currently-booked time, with no personal data in the URL and no internal participant identities shown.
2. **Given** the candidate picks an available new slot, **When** they confirm, **Then** the new slot is reserved atomically, new calendar events are created for all participants, the old events are cancelled, updated invites and a fresh confirmation are dispatched exactly once per recipient, the booking reflects the new time, and the candidate sees a success confirmation.
3. **Given** the reschedule computation yields zero compliant slots in the searched window, **When** the candidate opens the reschedule link, **Then** they see a clear "no alternative times are currently available — your recruiter will follow up" message, the **existing booking remains unchanged**, and the recruiter is notified that the candidate attempted to reschedule.
4. **Given** creation of the new calendar events fails after the retry policy is exhausted, **When** the reschedule is attempted, **Then** the reschedule is rolled back, the **original booking and its calendar events remain intact and valid**, and the candidate is told the change could not be completed and to try again or keep the original time — the candidate is never left without an interview.
5. **Given** a new slot has become unavailable since the page loaded (an interviewer's calendar changed), **When** the candidate confirms it, **Then** the system refuses that slot with a "no longer available" message, leaves the original booking intact, and offers the remaining valid slots.

### User Story 2 - Recruiter reschedules on behalf of any party (Priority: P2)

A recruiter needs to move an interview — an interviewer dropped out, or the candidate asked by phone. From the candidate's view in Cadence, the recruiter triggers a reschedule. Cadence re-runs availability and sends the candidate a fresh reschedule invitation, while the existing booking stays in place until the candidate confirms a new time, so the candidate is never left in limbo. The recruiter can see that a reschedule is in progress.

**Why this priority**: Recruiters must be able to act when a candidate can't or won't self-serve (e.g., an interviewer became unavailable). It is valuable but secondary to the candidate self-service path (Story 1), which removes the most volume; recruiter-initiated reschedule depends on the same reschedule machinery.

**Independent Test**: With a confirmed booking, a recruiter triggers "Reschedule" from the candidate view; confirm that a reschedule invitation is dispatched to the candidate, the candidate can pick a new slot, the booking moves on confirmation, and the recruiter view reflects "Reschedule in progress" then "Scheduled" at the new time.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a confirmed booking, **When** a recruiter (Admin or Recruiter role) triggers a reschedule from the candidate view, **Then** Cadence dispatches a fresh reschedule invitation to the candidate (consent-gated), keeps the existing booking active, and the recruiter sees the status as "Reschedule in progress."
2. **Given** a recruiter-triggered reschedule where the candidate then picks a new slot, **When** the candidate confirms, **Then** the booking moves to the new time with the same atomic swap and notifications as Story 1, and the recruiter view reads "Scheduled" at the new time.
3. **Given** a recruiter triggers a reschedule for a candidate who has become not-contactable (consent withdrawn, erased, over-retention, undeliverable), **When** the action is attempted, **Then** it is refused with a clear not-contactable reason and no reschedule invitation is dispatched.

### User Story 3 - Candidate cancels via the same link (Priority: P2)

A candidate can no longer attend and will not rebook. From the same confirmation email link, they choose "Cancel." Cadence releases the slot, removes the calendar events for all participants, notifies the recruiter that the candidate cancelled, and shows the candidate a respectful confirmation. The freed slot becomes available to others again.

**Why this priority**: Cancellation is the honest closure path and the precondition for slot reuse and waitlist defense (F23). It is simpler than reschedule but equally part of "either side can cancel via one link." It is independently testable: cancel a booking and verify slot release, event removal, and recruiter notification.

**Independent Test**: From a confirmed booking, open the cancel action, confirm cancellation, and verify that (a) the calendar events are removed from all attendees' calendars, (b) the slot is released and becomes bookable again, (c) the recruiter receives a cancellation notification, (d) the audit trail records the cancellation with initiator and timestamp.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a confirmed booking and a valid cancel link, **When** the candidate confirms cancellation, **Then** the calendar events are removed for every participant, the slot is released back to available, the recruiter is notified, the candidate sees a "your interview has been cancelled" confirmation, and the action is audit-logged.
2. **Given** a recruiter who needs to cancel an interview, **When** they cancel from the candidate view, **Then** the events are removed, the slot released, the candidate is notified of the cancellation, and the action is audit-logged with the recruiter as initiator.
3. **Given** removal of a participant's calendar event fails after retries during cancellation, **When** the cancellation is processed, **Then** the booking is recorded as cancelled with a distinct "cleanup-incomplete" state for the residual event (a surfaced, known orphan — never a silent leak), and the recruiter is alerted to remove it manually.

---

### Edge Cases

- **Reschedule attempt cap reached**: a candidate who has already rescheduled the configured maximum number of times (default 3 per booking) is refused further self-service reschedules; the self-service link is invalidated, the candidate is told to contact the recruiter, and the recruiter is notified of the abuse-threshold breach. The recruiter can still reschedule on the candidate's behalf.
- **Interview already in the past or already started**: a reschedule or cancel attempt for an interview whose start time has passed (or is within a configured no-self-service lead time before start) is refused with a clear "this interview can no longer be changed online — contact your recruiter" message; the booking is unchanged.
- **Expired reschedule/cancel link**: a credential that genuinely existed and has expired yields a distinct "expired" experience (410); a used/invalidated/unknown credential yields a single indistinguishable "invalid" response (400) so the system is never an existence or identity oracle.
- **Concurrent candidate-reschedule and recruiter-cancel (or two simultaneous confirmations)**: the booking has exactly one authoritative state transition at a time; whichever action commits first wins, and the other receives a clear "this interview was just changed/cancelled — refresh to see the latest" outcome. No double-booking and no inconsistent split state can result.
- **Candidate reschedules to a slot that needs a different pool quorum**: for an "any N of pool" panel, the new time re-selects a still-free qualifying quorum from the pool; the re-booked participant set may differ from the original, and that is acceptable as long as the quorum and rules are satisfied. If no qualifying quorum can be formed at the chosen time, the slot is refused.
- **Mixed-provider panel (Google + Microsoft 365)**: on reschedule, each participant's old event is cancelled on their own provider and a new one created on their own provider; a failure on any provider rolls the reschedule back, preserving the original booking. On cancellation, every provider's event is removed.
- **DST boundary on the new time**: a re-booked slot near a daylight-saving transition lands at the correct wall-clock time on every attendee's calendar and renders correctly in the candidate's displayed time zone.
- **Candidate erased between confirmation and a pending recruiter-initiated reschedule**: erasure of the candidate cancels the active booking and removes its calendar events as part of the erasure obligation; any in-flight reschedule invitation is invalidated, and no calendar event survives for an erased data subject.
- **Rapid double-click / replay of the confirm action**: a repeated confirmation of an already-completed reschedule or cancellation is a no-op that returns the existing result, never a second swap, duplicate events, or duplicate emails.
- **Reschedule that resolves to the originally-booked time**: choosing the same time as the current booking is treated as a no-op confirmation (no event churn, no duplicate notifications) rather than a cancel-and-rebook of the identical slot.
- **Token probing**: high-rate or invalid credential attempts are rate-limited and never reveal whether a credential exists or maps to a candidate identity.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Initiation (candidate self-service and recruiter-triggered)**

- **FR-001**: The system MUST allow the candidate to initiate a reschedule or a cancellation of their confirmed interview from a link delivered in their confirmation email, with no login, account, or app install required — authenticated solely by the link credential.
- **FR-002**: The system MUST allow a recruiter (Admin or Recruiter role) to initiate a reschedule or a cancellation on behalf of any party from the candidate view, enforced server-side by role.
- **FR-003**: A recruiter-initiated reschedule MUST dispatch a fresh reschedule invitation to the candidate (consent-gated) and MUST keep the existing booking active until the candidate confirms a new time, so the candidate is never without a booking during an in-progress reschedule.
- **FR-004**: The system MUST refuse a reschedule or cancellation, with a clear message and no change to the booking, when the interview start time has already passed or is within a configurable minimum-lead-time before start during which online self-service changes are disabled (default: self-service allowed up until the interview start time).
- **FR-005**: The system MUST limit the number of self-service reschedules per booking to a configurable maximum (default 3); on breach it MUST refuse further self-service reschedule, invalidate the self-service link, surface a "contact your recruiter" message to the candidate, and notify the recruiter of the breach. Recruiter-initiated reschedule MUST remain available after the candidate cap is reached. The reschedule count MUST increment exactly once per **successfully committed** reschedule (atomically with the swap commit), MUST NOT be consumed by a rolled-back, refused, or same-time no-op reschedule, and MUST be counted **cumulatively over the booking's lifetime** (it is not reset by a successful reschedule). The cap-breach "contact your recruiter" guidance MUST be delivered via the email notification to the known candidate; the response on the public link MUST remain the byte-identical "invalid" (400) response (FR-018) so the cap state is never an existence oracle.

**Reschedule computation & atomic swap**

- **FR-006**: On a reschedule, the system MUST compute fresh offered slots by re-reading real-time participant availability and re-applying the template rules (reusing the existing rule engine), and MUST exclude the currently-booked time from the offered set; it MUST NOT offer a slot that is not rule-compliant and genuinely free at computation time. When computing reschedule slots, the system MUST treat the original booking's own held time as **not** occupying its participants' availability or daily-interview-cap capacity (the interview is being moved, not added), so a reschedule is never falsely refused because a required interviewer is "at cap" or "busy" solely on account of the very interview being rescheduled.
- **FR-007**: The system MUST refuse a reschedule when zero compliant slots exist in the searched window, leave the existing booking unchanged, and surface an actionable "no alternative times available" message to the initiator. For a candidate-initiated reschedule the recruiter MUST be notified; for a recruiter-initiated reschedule the system MUST retain the original booking and surface "no alternatives available — original booking retained" to the recruiter rather than dispatching a dead invitation to the candidate.
- **FR-008**: Reschedule confirmation MUST be atomic and MUST re-validate, at confirmation time, that the newly-chosen slot is still compliant and that the required participant quorum (re-selecting an "any N of pool" quorum from still-free members) is still free; if not, it MUST refuse that slot, leave the original booking intact, and offer the remaining valid slots. Under simultaneous submissions for the same new slot, exactly one MUST succeed and the others MUST receive a "slot already taken" outcome with the remaining options — no double-booking.
- **FR-009**: The reschedule MUST preserve the original booking until the new booking is fully committed. The system MUST reserve the new slot and create the new calendar events **before** cancelling the old calendar events, so that a failure at any point leaves the candidate with a valid interview — either the original (on rollback) or the new one (on success), never neither and never both as live bookings. At every observable instant the booking MUST have exactly one authoritative live calendar-event set (the original XOR the new); a concurrent reader MUST never observe zero or two live bookings. The transient internal state in which both the original and the new reservations/events exist (after new-create, before old-cancel) is bounded by the recovery sweep (FR-023), during which the **original remains authoritative** until the swap commits; the moment the new events are confirmed-created on all providers is the durable commit boundary.
- **FR-010**: If creation of any new calendar event fails after the adapter's retry policy is exhausted, the system MUST roll the reschedule back — releasing the new slot reservation and removing any new events already created on any provider — and MUST leave the original booking and its events intact; the initiator MUST be told the change could not be completed.
- **FR-011**: On a successful reschedule, the system MUST cancel the old calendar events for every participant on their respective provider and create the new events carrying the interview title, correct time zone, and recruiter-provided location/dial-in details (no auto-generated video link). If cancelling an old event fails after retries (with the new booking already committed), the system MUST record a distinct "cleanup-incomplete" state for the residual old event (a surfaced, known orphan — never a silent leak), surface it to the recruiter, and MUST NOT report the reschedule as cleanly completed. The recruiter-provided location/dial-in (which may contain a dial-in PIN) MUST be handled per the established sensitive-field treatment: encrypted at rest if persisted on the reschedule session, never logged, never included in the candidate-facing payload, and decrypted transiently only into the calendar event details.

**Cancellation**

- **FR-012**: On a cancellation, the system MUST remove the calendar events for every participant on their respective provider, release the reserved slot back to available, and transition the booking to a cancelled state. If removing a participant's event fails after retries, the system MUST record the "cleanup-incomplete" state for the residual event and alert the recruiter rather than silently leaking it. Cancellation MUST be a state-changing action behind an **explicit candidate confirmation step**; it MUST NOT be triggered by mere link resolution (a GET / prefetch / email link-scanner following the link MUST NOT cancel an interview).
- **FR-013**: A candidate-initiated cancellation MUST notify the recruiter; a recruiter-initiated cancellation MUST notify the candidate (consent-gated). Internal participants MUST be made aware of the cancellation (at minimum via removal of their calendar event, and via the member/system email path where used). Each notification MUST be dispatched exactly once per recipient per cancellation.

**Notifications & idempotency**

- **FR-014**: On a successful reschedule, the system MUST dispatch updated calendar invites and a fresh confirmation to the candidate (consent-gated candidate email channel) and to each internal participant (non-consent-gated member/system email path), exactly once per recipient per reschedule, each guarded by a per-recipient idempotency key so retries and replays cannot produce duplicate sends. The per-recipient idempotency key MUST incorporate a **reschedule-round discriminator** (the reschedule-round or new-booking identity) so that distinct reschedule rounds — including one that returns the candidate to a time used in an earlier round — never collide on the email channel's idempotency guard and never suppress a legitimate send. A notification failure MUST NOT roll back the already-committed reschedule.
- **FR-015**: A repeated or replayed confirmation of an already-completed reschedule or cancellation MUST be a no-op that returns the existing result and triggers no additional calendar changes or emails.
- **FR-016**: The system MUST re-evaluate candidate contactability at the moment of a candidate-facing dispatch (reschedule invitation, fresh confirmation); an erased or otherwise not-contactable candidate MUST NOT receive a candidate-channel email, and a recruiter-initiated reschedule for such a candidate MUST be refused with a not-contactable reason.

**Credential, expiry & access**

- **FR-017**: The reschedule/cancel credential MUST be backed by a cryptographically random token of at least 128 bits of entropy not derived from candidate identity, stored only in hashed form; the link URL MUST contain no personal data or internal identifiers beyond the opaque token. The credential MUST be delivered in **every** confirmation email (the initial booking confirmation and each post-reschedule confirmation) and bound to exactly one booking. A successful reschedule MUST issue a fresh credential in the new confirmation and invalidate the consumed one (single-use per slot-pick), so each reschedule round uses a distinct credential.
- **FR-017a**: All candidate-facing reschedule and cancel operations MUST derive the target booking, candidate, participant set, and current slot **exclusively** from the server-side state bound to the validated credential. No booking, candidate, slot-ownership, or participant identifier supplied in the client request may override that binding, so one candidate's credential can never act on another candidate's booking (no IDOR / cross-booking action).
- **FR-017b**: Issuing a new reschedule invitation (candidate- or recruiter-initiated) MUST supersede any prior still-live reschedule session/credential for the same booking, so at most one reschedule session is authoritative at a time; opening a superseded reschedule link MUST yield the indistinguishable "invalid" (400) response (FR-018).
- **FR-018**: The reschedule/cancel credential MUST become unusable once the booking is cancelled, once the reschedule cap is reached, or once the interview is no longer eligible for online change (FR-004); an expired credential MUST yield a distinct "expired" response (HTTP 410 Gone) while used/invalidated/unknown credentials MUST yield a single indistinguishable "invalid" response (HTTP 400) — never an existence or identity oracle. Any *helpful* refusal messaging for a still-valid-but-action-refused state (cap reached, cancelled, lead-time-ineligible) MUST be shown only **after** the credential itself authenticates successfully (an authenticated state page), never on the bare token-validation response — so a probing request for an unknown or used token cannot be distinguished from a cap-breached or cancelled one.
- **FR-019**: The credential validation and reschedule/cancel endpoints MUST be rate-limited per source IP (default 10 requests/minute, returning HTTP 429 on breach) to resist brute-force/enumeration, consistent with the candidate-link policy.
- **FR-020**: The candidate-facing reschedule and cancel pages MUST meet the same candidate-experience bar as the scheduling page (no login; mobile-first; WCAG 2.2 AA with zero accessibility violations; loads in under 2 seconds on a 4G connection; all user-facing strings localizable), reusing the established candidate-page standards. They MUST display interview times in the candidate's local time zone with DST-correct labels and MUST NOT expose internal participant identities.

**Status, audit & recovery**

- **FR-021**: The system MUST expose the per-candidate booking status to authorized recruiters, including at least: Scheduled (with time), Reschedule in progress, Rescheduled (with new time), and Cancelled — with the relevant timestamps.
- **FR-022**: The system MUST audit-log every reschedule and cancellation as an append-only entry recording the initiator (candidate or the specific recruiter), the old slot, the new slot (for a reschedule), the booking and candidate by internal identifier only, and the timestamp — with no token value and no personal data in the log. These append-only entries (PII-free: instants + internal identifiers only) MUST survive a subsequent candidate erasure unmodified — erasure wipes candidate PII fields only and MUST NOT delete or alter the append-only audit records, consistent with the audit-immutability guarantee.
- **FR-023**: A reschedule left half-applied by an interrupted confirmation (e.g., a crash after the new slot is reserved but before commit) MUST be automatically resolved by a recovery sweep so a stuck reschedule never permanently blocks a slot or corrupts the booking. The sweep's forward-vs-rollback decision MUST be **deterministic**, driven by the durable commit boundary (FR-009): if the new calendar events were confirmed-created on all participants' providers, the sweep MUST roll **forward** (cancel the old events, finalize the new booking); otherwise it MUST roll **back** (release the new reservation, remove any partially-created new events, retain the original booking). The original booking MUST never be left cancelled without a committed new booking. The recovery sweep MUST be idempotent and missed-fire-safe (reusing the shared scheduler-checkpoint pattern).
- **FR-024**: Erasure of a candidate MUST cancel any active booking for that candidate and remove its calendar events — including the events and reservation of any **in-flight** new reschedule round — release all associated reservations, and invalidate any in-flight reschedule/cancel credentials and invitations, so no calendar event or usable link survives for an erased data subject. Erasure-triggered calendar-event removal runs on the **asynchronous** erasure-completion path (it MUST NOT block the erasure acknowledgement) and inherits the "cleanup-incomplete" honest-bound on a provider failure rather than blocking erasure.

**Privacy & access control**

- **FR-025**: All recruiter-facing initiation and status endpoints MUST enforce role-based access server-side; candidate-facing reschedule/cancel endpoints MUST remain public-by-token only. Recruiter endpoints MUST be **workspace-scoped**: a reschedule, cancellation, or status read for a booking outside the recruiter's workspace MUST return the same indistinguishable scoped not-found response as a non-existent booking (no cross-workspace existence oracle).
- **FR-026**: No personal data (candidate name, email, phone) may appear in application logs at any level; only internal identifiers and opaque references are permitted.
- **FR-027**: A reschedule confirmation that resolves to the **currently-booked instant** MUST be treated as an idempotent no-op that returns the existing booking — it MUST NOT churn calendar events, dispatch notifications, consume the credential, or increment the reschedule count. This same-time short-circuit MUST be evaluated **before** any new slot reservation is attempted, so the booking's own held slot is never treated as a competing reservation (which would otherwise self-collide on the reservation guard and falsely report "slot already taken").

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Booking (Confirmed Interview)** *(extended from F13)*: gains a reschedule-lifecycle state (Scheduled, Reschedule in progress, Rescheduled, Cancelled), a reschedule counter (against the configurable cap), references to the current calendar events, and the history needed to audit old-vs-new slots. The unit whose state the reschedule/cancel actions mutate.
- **Reschedule/Cancel Credential** *(extends the F13 single-use token model)*: the candidate's keyed, hashed, expiring credential bound to one booking, granting access to that booking's reschedule slot-picker and cancel action; re-issued on each successful reschedule and invalidated on cancel, cap-breach, or ineligibility.
- **Reschedule Session**: one in-progress attempt to move a booking — holds the freshly-computed offered slots (excluding the current time), the initiator, and the credential state for that round; resolved to a new booking, a no-op, or a refusal.
- **Slot Reservation** *(reused from F13)*: the atomic per-participant-time reservation guard; reused so the new time is held before the old events are cancelled and a released old slot becomes bookable again.
- **Audit Entry** *(reused/extended)*: append-only record of each reschedule and cancellation, keyed by candidate (internal identifier only), capturing initiator, old slot, new slot, and timestamp — free of personal data and token values.
- **Recruiter Notification**: the in-app/email signal raised on candidate-initiated cancel, candidate reschedule with no available slots, and reschedule cap-breach, so the recruiter can act.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A candidate can complete a reschedule (open link → pick new slot → confirmed) in under 2 minutes with no login, and a human-initiated reschedule (candidate or recruiter) costs the human under 60 seconds of active effort.
- **SC-002**: Every successful reschedule results in the old calendar events appearing as cancelled and new events appearing at the new time on all attendees' calendars within 30 seconds of confirmation (measured against the test provider harness).
- **SC-003**: In 100% of failed reschedules (new-event creation fails after retries, chosen slot no longer free, or zero alternative slots), the original booking and its calendar events remain intact and valid — the candidate is never left without an interview.
- **SC-004**: Under genuinely simultaneous conflicting actions on one booking (two reschedule confirmations of the same new slot, or a reschedule racing a cancel) — exercised by a gated/latched concurrent test repeated across multiple trials — exactly one action commits and all others are cleanly refused, with no double-booking and no inconsistent split state, 100% of the time.
- **SC-005**: On a mid-reschedule or mid-cancellation provider failure, the result is either a complete rollback/clean cancellation with zero orphaned events or a surfaced "cleanup-incomplete" state — never a silent orphan and never a clean-success report alongside an orphaned event.
- **SC-006**: A reschedule to a slot within one hour of a DST transition lands at the correct wall-clock time on every attendee's calendar (verified against the recorded event payload's UTC offset and IANA time zone) and renders correctly in the candidate's displayed time zone.
- **SC-007**: After a candidate exhausts the configured reschedule cap, 100% of further self-service reschedule attempts are refused with a "contact your recruiter" message, the link is invalidated, and the recruiter is notified — while recruiter-initiated reschedule still succeeds.
- **SC-008**: An expired reschedule/cancel link produces a helpful, distinct "expired" (410) experience in 100% of expired-link accesses, while used/invalid/unknown credentials are indistinguishable from one another (no existence oracle), and credential endpoints are rate-limited (429 on breach).
- **SC-009**: No reschedule invitation or confirmation email is dispatched for a candidate who fails the contactability check (consent missing/withdrawn/erased/over-retention/undeliverable) in 100% of such cases, and erasure of a candidate cancels their booking and removes its calendar events with zero residual events for the erased subject.
- **SC-010**: Every reschedule and cancellation is represented by exactly one append-only audit entry recording initiator, old slot, new slot (for reschedules), and timestamp, free of personal data and token values; zero occurrences of candidate personal data or token values appear in application logs across the full reschedule/cancel flow (verified by log scan).
- **SC-011**: A candidate cancellation releases the slot such that it becomes selectable again (immediately, in the next computation after the cancel commits) by a subsequent scheduling/reschedule computation in 100% of cancellations, and the recruiter receives exactly one cancellation notification.
- **SC-012**: The required end-to-end reschedule test passes as a single automated flow: candidate opens the reschedule link → picks a new slot → the old calendar events are cancelled and new ones created on all attendees' calendars → updated invites are dispatched → exactly one append-only audit entry records the old slot, new slot, initiator, and timestamp.
- **SC-013**: A rolled-back, refused, or same-time no-op reschedule does NOT consume a reschedule-count attempt — the cap reflects only successfully committed reschedules — verified by driving a failing/no-op reschedule and confirming the remaining reschedule budget is unchanged.
- **SC-014**: A valid credential bound to one booking cannot act on any other booking: a request supplying a different booking, candidate, or slot identifier never affects a booking other than the credential-bound one, in 100% of such attempts (no IDOR), and recruiter reads/actions for an out-of-workspace booking return the indistinguishable scoped not-found response.

## Assumptions

- **Dependencies are complete and reused**: F13 single-stage scheduling (atomic reservation, single-use token model, audit trail), F10/F11 calendar adapters (event create/cancel, rollback, cleanup-incomplete handling), F12 rule engine (slot computation), F21/F22 email (template rendering, consent-gated dispatch, member/system mail path), F04 GDPR primitives (contactability gate, shared erasure wipe, candidate audit taxonomy including the `BOOKING_CHANGED` forward contract), and F00.2 scheduler-checkpoint pattern. F20 orchestrates and extends these rather than reimplementing them.
- **Email-only notifications**: reschedule/cancel notifications and invitations ride the existing email channel. SMS/WhatsApp nudges remain deferred to v1.5.
- **No automatic meeting-link generation** (OD-1 carried forward): the interview location/dial-in remains recruiter-provided free text carried onto the re-booked calendar event.
- **Single-stage only**: reschedule/cancel applies to single-stage Flow A1 bookings. Multi-stage "loop" reschedule (Flow A2) is out of scope and deferred to v1.5.
- **No-show defense (F23) is a separate feature**: the 24-hour confirmation cascade, one-tap slot release, and waitlist mechanics are owned by F23. F20 provides the cancellation/slot-release primitive F23 builds on but does not implement the confirmation cascade; F20 must not preclude it.
- **Recruiter-initiated reschedule re-invites the candidate** (preserving the existing booking until a new slot is confirmed) rather than letting the recruiter pick a slot directly on the candidate's behalf; direct recruiter slot-selection is not required for the MVP and is out of scope.
- **Reschedule cap default is 3 per booking**, configurable per workspace, per the backlog abuse-prevention requirement (BA AC-2). The cap counts candidate self-service reschedules; recruiter-initiated reschedules are not capped.
- **Self-service lead-time cutoff default is none** (online reschedule/cancel allowed up until the interview start time); a configurable minimum-lead-time may disable self-service closer to the interview, with last-minute changes routed to the recruiter.
- **Reschedule/cancel credential delivery**: the credential is delivered in every confirmation email (the initial F13 confirmation and each post-reschedule confirmation), bound to the booking, and re-issued single-use per reschedule round, consistent with the backlog's "reschedule token TTL 72 h from send; single-use after new slot confirmed" and the F14 token requirements (410 expired / 400 invalid / 429 rate-limited).
- **Candidate-facing UX standards** for the reschedule and cancel pages reuse the F14 candidate-scheduling-page standards (WCAG 2.2 AA via axe, mobile-first, < 2 s on 4G, Lighthouse performance ≥ 85, `$localize` for all strings). This feature must meet that bar on its new candidate surfaces, not merely "not preclude" it.
- **Full pipeline view / bulk actions remain F51**: F20 surfaces the reschedule/cancel actions and the per-candidate booking status on the existing recruiter candidate view; the multi-candidate board and bulk reschedule/cancel are F51. The backlog US-F20-2 "from the pipeline view" is satisfied by surfacing the action on this per-candidate recruiter view; the colour-coded multi-candidate board is F51.
- **Candidate status page is F30**: rendering the new/rescheduled/cancelled state on the candidate's own status page is F30's responsibility. F20 mutates the booking state F30 reads and must not preclude it; the post-cancel candidate confirmation directs the candidate appropriately rather than dead-ending.
- **"Reschedule in progress" is a derived state, not a mutation of the live booking**: a reschedule round is modelled as a distinct request/session document referencing the still-live booking (the F13 supersession precedent); the booking's authoritative state remains "booked" until the swap commits (FR-009). The new append-only audit event values (reschedule / cancellation, with initiator and outcome) are enumerated in `data-model.md` under the F04 audit taxonomy (the F13 `SCHEDULING_*` precedent — add-only, never reorder), reconciling the reserved `BOOKING_CHANGED` forward contract.
- **Token primitives reused from F13**: the credential reuses the existing 256-bit secure-token generator and HMAC token-hasher (well above the 128-bit floor in FR-017) and the F13 atomic request-status CAS for single-winner concurrency — not a new locking mechanism.
- **Recruiter notifications** ride the existing recruiter notification channel (in-app, with the member/system email path where applicable, per the F22 precedent) — not a new notification system.
- **Inherited concurrency bound (carried from F13)**: the exact-start-time reservation guard fully serializes same-time and same-booking contention (two confirmations of the same new slot, reschedule vs cancel on one booking); a sub-second cross-template partial-overlap window remains an accepted MVP limit, as documented for F13.
- **MongoDB indexes** for the booking-state and reschedule-credential lookups, the reused slot-reservation reservation guard, and the half-applied-reschedule recovery scan are declared in `plan.md` per the F00.1 pattern; the new recovery-sweep `@Scheduled` predicate MUST have a covering index (F00.2: no scheduled task queries an uncovered collection). **No new infrastructure service, broker, or queue is introduced** (constitution C2), and no dependency outside the fixed stack is added (C4).
