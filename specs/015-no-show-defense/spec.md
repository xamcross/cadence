> Backlog reference: **F23 — Flow A4: No-Show Defense** (Tier 2 — Required MVP, P2). Spec refs: product spec §4 Flow A4, §5.1 (FR-6). The second item in the post-F13 "Resilience" branch of the delivery sequence, alongside F20 (Reschedule & Cancellation), which is now complete. F23 builds the confirmation cascade on top of the F13 single-stage booking, reusing the F20 cancellation/slot-release primitive, the F22 consent-gated email channel and recruiter-notification path, the F21 reminder templates (`REMINDER_24H`/`REMINDER_1H` already exist), the F14 candidate-page standards, the F04 contactability/erasure primitives, and the F00.2 scheduler-checkpoint pattern for the timed `@Scheduled` cascade.
>
> **MVP scope decisions carried forward (backlog v1.2.0)**: the cascade is **email-only** — the SMS/WhatsApp nudge step from product spec §4 Flow A4 is **deferred to v1.5** and MUST NOT exist in the MVP codebase. **Waitlisted-candidate auto-invitation** (the "invite waitlisted candidate" half of product-spec FR-6) is **not in the F23 backlog acceptance criteria** and is deferred: F23 ships one-tap slot **release** (the released slot becomes available to normal scheduling/reschedule again); automatic waitlist invitation is out of scope. **No-show analytics rendering** is owned by **F50 (Core Dashboard)**: F23 records the data and defines the no-show signal; the dashboard widget is F50.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Candidate confirms attendance from a reminder (Priority: P1)

A candidate with a confirmed interview receives, a configurable lead time before the interview (default 24 hours), a branded reminder email asking them to confirm they will attend. They tap "Confirm attendance" on a mobile-friendly page — no login, no account — and immediately see a clear "Thanks, you're confirmed" acknowledgement. Cadence records the confirmation so the recruiter knows the candidate is committed and the slot will not silently go to waste.

**Why this priority**: Surfacing a candidate's intent to attend *before* the interview is the core of no-show defense and the precondition for every downstream action (escalation, release). It is independently demonstrable from a single confirmed booking: fire the cascade, follow the link, confirm, and observe the recorded confirmation. It delivers value on its own — a confirmed candidate is a reassured recruiter — even if the escalation path (Story 2) is not yet built.

**Independent Test**: From a confirmed booking, advance the test clock to the confirmation-request lead time, observe a single consent-gated reminder email dispatched to the candidate, open the confirm link with no authentication, confirm attendance, and verify the booking records the confirmation exactly once and the candidate sees an accessible success acknowledgement — with no personal data in the URL.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a confirmed booking whose start is at or beyond the configured confirmation-request lead time, **When** the scheduled cascade runs at the lead-time boundary, **Then** exactly one reminder email asking the candidate to confirm attendance is dispatched to the candidate over the consent-gated channel, carrying a link to a confirm page, and the booking is marked "confirmation requested" with a timestamp.
2. **Given** a candidate with a valid confirmation link, **When** they open it and explicitly confirm attendance, **Then** the booking is recorded as "candidate confirmed" with a timestamp, the candidate sees a clear localized success acknowledgement in their local time zone, and no internal participant identities are exposed.
3. **Given** a candidate who already confirmed, **When** the confirm action is repeated (double-tap, refresh, replay), **Then** it is an idempotent no-op that returns the existing "confirmed" acknowledgement and dispatches no additional email and raises no additional recruiter signal.
4. **Given** a booking that was rescheduled to a new time after a confirmation was requested or recorded, **When** the cascade next runs, **Then** the confirmation state is reset for the new time and a fresh confirmation request is sent at the new time's lead-time boundary (a confirmation for an old time never counts toward a new time).

---

### User Story 2 - Recruiter is alerted to an unconfirmed interview and can release the slot in one tap (Priority: P1)

A candidate has not confirmed attendance by the configured deadline. Cadence alerts the recruiter that the interview is unconfirmed and gives them a one-tap action to release the slot. When the recruiter releases it, Cadence removes the calendar events for all participants, frees the slot so it can be scheduled again, and records the release — turning a likely no-show into a recovered slot instead of wasted panel time.

**Why this priority**: The recruiter alert + one-tap release is what actually defends against no-shows; a confirmation request with no escalation is just a reminder. It is co-P1 with Story 1 because the backlog's defining value ("turn an unconfirmed interview into a recovered slot") requires both. It is independently testable: drive a booking to the unconfirmed deadline without a confirmation and verify the recruiter alert and the one-tap release behaviour.

**Independent Test**: From a confirmed booking with no candidate confirmation, advance the test clock past the configured escalation deadline, verify exactly one recruiter alert is raised for the unconfirmed interview, invoke the one-tap release as the recruiter, and confirm the calendar events are removed for all participants, the slot is released and becomes bookable again, and the booking transitions to a released state recorded in the audit trail.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a booking with a confirmation requested but no candidate confirmation, **When** the scheduled cascade runs at or after the configured escalation deadline, **Then** exactly one "interview unconfirmed" alert is raised to the recruiter (idempotent across sweeps and missed fires), and the booking is marked "unconfirmed — escalated" with a timestamp.
2. **Given** an unconfirmed escalated interview, **When** the recruiter taps "release slot," **Then** the calendar events are removed for every participant on their respective provider, the reserved slot is released back to available, the booking transitions to a released/cancelled state, the action is audit-logged with the recruiter as initiator, and the freed time is selectable again by a subsequent scheduling/reschedule computation.
3. **Given** an interview the candidate confirms *after* the recruiter was already alerted but *before* the recruiter releases the slot, **When** the recruiter views the booking, **Then** the booking now reads "candidate confirmed" and the recruiter is not pushed to release a now-confirmed interview; releasing remains possible but is no longer prompted.
4. **Given** a candidate who confirmed attendance, **When** the escalation deadline passes, **Then** no unconfirmed alert is raised for that booking.

---

### User Story 3 - Admin configures the confirmation cascade per workspace (Priority: P2)

An admin tunes the no-show defense to their hiring rhythm: how far before an interview the confirmation request goes out, and the deadline after which an unconfirmed interview escalates to the recruiter. The workspace's settings drive every booking's cascade without per-interview configuration.

**Why this priority**: Sensible defaults make the cascade work out of the box (Stories 1 and 2 stand alone on the defaults), so configuration is valuable but secondary. It is independently testable by changing the workspace settings and verifying the cascade timings shift accordingly.

**Independent Test**: Set a non-default confirmation-request lead time and escalation deadline on a workspace, create a booking, and verify the reminder email and the recruiter escalation fire at the newly configured offsets (using the test clock), not the defaults.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an admin in workspace settings, **When** they set the confirmation-request lead time and the unconfirmed-escalation deadline (within allowed bounds), **Then** the settings persist, survive restart, and apply to all subsequent cascade evaluations in that workspace.
2. **Given** a workspace that has never customized these settings, **When** a booking's cascade runs, **Then** the documented defaults apply (confirmation request at 24 hours before start; escalation when still unconfirmed at the default deadline before start).
3. **Given** an admin sets values that would make the escalation deadline fall before the confirmation-request lead time (a contradictory window), **When** they save, **Then** the system rejects the configuration with a clear validation message and the prior valid settings remain in effect.

### Edge Cases

- **Booking made inside the lead-time window**: an interview booked fewer hours before its start than the confirmation-request lead time (e.g., booked 3 hours out with a 24-hour lead) still gets a confirmation request at the next cascade sweep (sent as soon as possible), and its escalation deadline is evaluated against the (possibly already-past) offset — collapsing to "request immediately, escalate at/after the deadline" without skipping the cascade entirely.
- **Interview start reached with no confirmation and no release**: the booking is recorded as a no-show signal (unconfirmed at start) for analytics (F50), even if the recruiter never tapped release; Cadence cannot observe physical attendance, so "unconfirmed at start" is the MVP no-show proxy and is defined explicitly.
- **Candidate not contactable when the confirmation request would fire**: if the candidate fails the contactability check (consent withdrawn, erased, over-retention, undeliverable) at request time, no candidate-channel email is sent; the interview cannot self-confirm and escalates to the recruiter at the deadline via the **same coarse "interview unconfirmed" alert** as any other unconfirmed booking — the recruiter is never told *why* the confirmation could not be requested (no GDPR/contactability oracle), only that the interview is unconfirmed and can be released.
- **One candidate with multiple live bookings**: a candidate holding more than one live confirmed booking runs an **independent** cascade per booking — each booking gets its own confirmation request, its own confirm credential bound to that one booking, and its own escalation. Confirming one booking never confirms another; the per-booking idempotency keys keep the cascades isolated.
- **Candidate confirms, then the booking is cancelled**: a booking the candidate confirmed and that is subsequently cancelled (by the candidate, recruiter, or erasure via F20) is recorded as **cancelled, not as a no-show** — the no-show signal (FR-016) is for interviews that reached their start unconfirmed, and a confirmed-then-cancelled booking is excluded from the no-show count.
- **Interview start passes during an escalation sweep**: a booking whose start instant passes mid-sweep transitions to "no longer live"; the escalation stage MUST NOT raise a fresh unconfirmed alert for an interview that is already past — the start-reached stage stamps the no-show signal instead.
- **Candidate confirms after the slot was already released/cancelled**: a confirmation against a booking that is no longer live (released, cancelled, rescheduled-away, or past) yields the candidate-link "no longer active / contact your recruiter" experience — it does not resurrect a released booking.
- **Reschedule between request and confirmation**: a reschedule (F20) supersedes the confirmation state; the new booking round starts its own cascade for the new time, and a stale confirm link for the old time yields the indistinguishable "invalid" response.
- **Cancellation between request and deadline**: a cancelled booking (F20, candidate/recruiter/erasure) raises no unconfirmed escalation and the confirm link becomes unusable.
- **Erasure mid-cascade**: erasure of a candidate halts the cascade (no further confirmation request or escalation email), invalidates the confirm credential, and the existing erasure obligation cancels the booking and removes its calendar events; no usable confirm link survives for an erased data subject.
- **DST boundary on the lead-time / deadline calculation**: the lead-time and deadline offsets are computed against the interview's absolute start instant so the cascade fires at the correct wall-clock moment across a daylight-saving transition (the offset never drifts by an hour).
- **Missed scheduler fire (deploy/restart/outage)**: a cascade window that should have fired during downtime is replayed on recovery exactly once per booking per stage — neither double-sent nor silently skipped (the F00.2 checkpoint contract).
- **Confirm-link prefetch / scanner**: an email security scanner or browser prefetch that merely *follows* the confirm link (a GET) MUST NOT record a confirmation; confirmation requires an explicit candidate action, so a scanner cannot produce a false "confirmed."
- **Two recruiters release the same slot simultaneously**: exactly one release commits; the second is a no-op on an already-released booking ("this interview was just released — refresh"). No double cancellation, no duplicate notifications.
- **Token probing**: high-rate or invalid confirm-credential attempts are rate-limited and never reveal whether a credential exists or maps to a candidate identity (consistent with the candidate-link policy; 410 expired vs 400 invalid, 429 on breach).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**Terminology.** A **live confirmed booking** for the purposes of the cascade is a booking currently in the booked state for an interview whose start is in the future and that has not been cancelled, released, or superseded by a committed reschedule. During an in-flight reschedule round (F20), the original booking remains the live, authoritative booking until the swap commits (per F20's commit boundary); the cascade always keys off whichever booking is currently the authoritative booked round for the current interview start.

**Confirmation cascade (timed dispatch)**

- **FR-001**: The system MUST, for every live confirmed booking, dispatch a single candidate-facing attendance-confirmation reminder when the interview's start reaches the workspace-configured confirmation-request lead time (default: 24 hours before start), reusing the existing reminder template (`REMINDER_24H`) and the consent-gated candidate email channel. The reminder MUST contain a link to a confirm page bearing only an opaque token.
- **FR-002**: The cascade MUST run on the shared `@Scheduled` + scheduler-checkpoint pattern (F00.2): idempotent and missed-fire-safe, so a duplicate sweep, an overlapping run, or a restart that crosses a cascade boundary never produces a duplicate confirmation request or a duplicate recruiter escalation, and a window that should have fired during downtime is replayed exactly once on recovery. The cascade MUST drive three per-booking stages — (1) **confirmation request** at the lead-time boundary, (2) **unconfirmed escalation** at the escalation deadline, and (3) **no-show-signal** when the interview start is reached (FR-016) — each advanced by a per-booking-per-stage atomic guard (so correctness rests on the per-booking guard, not single-threaded execution), and each candidate-facing dispatch guarded by a per-booking-per-stage idempotency key so retries cannot double-send. Because the sweep runs at a fixed interval, a stage MUST fire within one sweep interval of its boundary (a fixed bounded lateness, not an indefinite drift); a stage boundary crossed between sweeps is honoured at the next sweep.
- **FR-003**: The cascade MUST fire against the booking's **current** interview start instant, and the per-stage idempotency key MUST incorporate the current interview start/round identity. A booking that was rescheduled (F20) MUST reset its confirmation state and run a fresh cascade for the new time; a confirmation recorded for a superseded time MUST NOT satisfy the confirmation requirement for the new time, and a re-sent confirmation request after a reschedule MUST NOT be suppressed by a stale idempotency key from the prior round.
- **FR-004**: For an interview booked fewer hours before its start than the configured lead time, the confirmation request MUST still be dispatched at the next sweep (as soon as possible) rather than skipped, and the escalation MUST still be evaluated against the configured deadline; if the escalation deadline is already in the past at booking time, the escalation MUST fire at the next sweep after the request has been made (request-then-escalate ordering is preserved even when both boundaries are already past).
- **FR-005**: The cascade MUST evaluate candidate contactability at the moment the confirmation request would be dispatched; if the candidate is not contactable (consent missing/withdrawn, erased, over-retention, undeliverable), the system MUST NOT send a candidate-channel email and MUST escalate that booking to the recruiter at the deadline as unconfirmed (never a silent gap). The recruiter signal for such a booking MUST be the **same coarse "interview unconfirmed" alert** as a candidate-non-response (FR-010) and MUST NOT disclose *why* the confirmation could not be requested (erased vs withdrawn vs over-retention vs undeliverable), so the alert is never a candidate-contactability or GDPR-state oracle to the recruiter; any "could not auto-request" distinction is an internal, value-free state only.

**Candidate confirmation**

- **FR-006**: The candidate MUST be able to confirm attendance from the reminder link with no login, account, or app install — authenticated solely by the link credential — and confirmation MUST be a **state-changing action behind an explicit candidate confirmation step** (a non-GET request, e.g. a deliberate "Confirm attendance" submission), NOT triggered by mere link resolution: a GET / prefetch / email link-scanner that merely follows the link MUST NOT record a confirmation.
- **FR-007**: A successful confirmation MUST record "candidate confirmed" with a timestamp on the booking, exactly once; a repeated or replayed confirmation MUST be an idempotent no-op that returns the existing confirmed acknowledgement and triggers no additional email or recruiter signal.
- **FR-008**: All candidate-facing confirmation operations MUST derive the target booking, candidate, and interview **exclusively** from the server-side state bound to the validated credential; no booking, candidate, or slot identifier supplied in the client request may override that binding (no IDOR / cross-booking action).
- **FR-009**: A confirmation against a booking that is no longer live (released, cancelled, rescheduled-away, expired, or already past) MUST NOT resurrect or alter the booking; it MUST yield the candidate-link "no longer active" experience and direct the candidate to contact their recruiter.

**Escalation & one-tap release**

- **FR-010**: The cascade MUST, when a live booking remains unconfirmed at or after the workspace-configured escalation deadline (default: 2 hours before start), raise exactly one "interview unconfirmed" alert to the recruiter through the existing recruiter-notification channel (value-free; no candidate personal data; booking and candidate referenced by internal identifier only), and mark the booking "unconfirmed — escalated" with a timestamp. The alert MUST NOT be raised for a booking the candidate has confirmed, nor for a cancelled/rescheduled-away/released booking.
- **FR-011**: The recruiter (Admin or Recruiter role) MUST be able to release the slot of an unconfirmed (or any live) booking in a single action, enforced server-side by role and workspace scope. The release MUST be an **authenticated in-app, non-GET state change**: any "one-tap" affordance in an alert email MUST land the recruiter on an authenticated in-app confirmation and MUST NOT execute the destructive release on a bare link-GET (so a forwarded email, prefetch, or scanner cannot tear down calendar events). Releasing MUST reuse the established F20 recruiter-initiated cancellation / slot-release primitive: remove the calendar events for every participant on their respective provider, release the reserved slot back to available, transition the booking to a released/cancelled state with a distinct "released as unconfirmed / no-show" reason (for the FR-016 analytics signal), notify the candidate of the cancellation over the consent-gated channel (consistent with the reused F20 recruiter-cancel behaviour), and audit-log the release with the recruiter as initiator.
- **FR-012**: A released slot MUST become selectable again (immediately, in the next computation after the release commits) by a subsequent scheduling or reschedule computation. If removing a participant's event fails after retries, the system MUST record the established "cleanup-incomplete" state for the residual event and alert the recruiter rather than silently leaking it (no clean-success report alongside an orphan).
- **FR-013**: A release action MUST be idempotent and single-winner under concurrency: simultaneous releases of the same booking, or a release racing a candidate confirmation or an F20 cancel/reschedule, MUST result in exactly one authoritative state transition; the losers MUST receive a clear "already changed — refresh" outcome with no double cancellation and no duplicate notifications.

**Configuration**

- **FR-014**: An Admin MUST be able to configure, per workspace, the confirmation-request lead time and the unconfirmed-escalation deadline (both expressed as a positive duration before the interview start), persisted in workspace settings, surviving restart, and applied to all subsequent cascade evaluations in that workspace. The system MUST reject — retaining the prior valid settings — any configuration where the escalation deadline is not strictly between the interview start and the confirmation-request lead time; that is, the request lead time MUST be the larger offset (further before start) and the escalation deadline MUST be a smaller positive offset (after the request, before start), so the ordering request → escalation → start always holds.
- **FR-015**: The system MUST apply documented defaults when a workspace has not customized these settings: confirmation request at **24 hours** before start, and unconfirmed escalation at **2 hours** before start. The cascade MUST function correctly on the defaults with no admin action required.

**No-show signal (data for F50)**

- **FR-016**: The system MUST record, per booking, a no-show signal when the interview start instant is reached without a candidate confirmation (the MVP no-show proxy, since physical attendance is not observable to Cadence). This signal MUST be stamped by the cascade's start-reached stage (FR-002), not inferred only at read time, so it exists even when the recruiter never tapped release. The system MUST distinguish a confirmed interview, an unconfirmed-escalated interview, a recruiter-released interview, and a booking that was confirmed and then later cancelled (a confirmed-then-cancelled booking MUST NOT be counted as a no-show). These records MUST be sufficient for a later dashboard (F50) to compute a no-show rate for a window; F23 records the data and MUST NOT preclude that rendering.

**Credential, expiry & access**

- **FR-017**: The confirm credential MUST be backed by a cryptographically random token of at least 128 bits of entropy not derived from candidate identity, stored only in hashed form; the link URL MUST contain no personal data or internal identifiers beyond the opaque token, and the credential MUST be bound to exactly one booking. The credential's natural lifetime runs until the interview start instant: once the interview start has passed, the credential is **expired**. Resolving/opening the link (read) MUST be idempotent and is NOT consumed — a candidate may open the link multiple times before confirming; only the **positive confirmation** consumes it (single-use for the confirm action). The credential MUST also become unusable once the booking is released, cancelled, or superseded by a committed reschedule.
- **FR-018**: The confirm credential and its validation endpoint MUST follow the established candidate-link policy: a credential whose interview start has passed yields a distinct "expired" response (HTTP 410 Gone), while used/invalidated/unknown credentials yield a single indistinguishable "invalid" response (HTTP 400) — never an existence or identity oracle — and the endpoints MUST be rate-limited per source IP (default 10 requests/minute, HTTP 429 on breach). Any *helpful* state messaging for a still-valid-but-action-refused condition (e.g. "already confirmed," "this interview was cancelled") MUST be shown only **after** the credential itself authenticates successfully (an authenticated state page), never on the bare token-validation response — so a probe for an unknown/used token cannot be distinguished from an already-confirmed or cancelled one.
- **FR-019**: The candidate-facing confirm page MUST meet the same candidate-experience bar as the scheduling page (F14): no login; mobile-first; WCAG 2.2 AA with zero accessibility violations; loads in under 2 seconds on a 4G connection; all user-facing strings localizable; interview time shown in the candidate's local time zone with DST-correct labelling; and MUST NOT expose internal participant identities.

**Privacy, audit & access control**

- **FR-020**: All recruiter-facing escalation, status, and release endpoints MUST enforce role-based access server-side and be workspace-scoped: a release or status read for a booking outside the recruiter's workspace MUST return the same indistinguishable scoped not-found response as a non-existent booking (no cross-workspace existence oracle). Candidate-facing confirm endpoints MUST remain public-by-token only.
- **FR-021**: Every attendance confirmation, unconfirmed escalation, and slot release MUST be written as an append-only audit entry recording the actor (the candidate, the system cascade, or the specific recruiter), the booking and candidate by internal identifier only, and the timestamp — with no token value and no personal data. These entries MUST survive a subsequent candidate erasure unmodified (erasure wipes candidate PII fields only).
- **FR-022**: No personal data (candidate name, email, phone) and no token value may appear in application logs at any level across the full cascade, confirmation, escalation, and release flow; only internal identifiers and opaque references are permitted, and recruiter notifications MUST remain value-free.

**Scope guard**

- **FR-023**: The MVP cascade MUST be **email-only**. No SMS or WhatsApp dispatch path, dependency, or configuration may exist in the codebase (the deferred-to-v1.5 nudge step). Automatic waitlisted-candidate invitation MUST NOT be implemented and no waitlist data model exists in the MVP; one-tap slot release returning the slot to general availability is the only recovery action in scope (F23 implements only the "slot-release" half of product-spec FR-6).

**Erasure interaction**

- **FR-024**: Erasure of a candidate MUST halt the cascade for that candidate's bookings (no further confirmation request or escalation email) and MUST invalidate the confirm credential atomically with the erasure wipe (no race in which a sweep fires between the wipe and the credential invalidation). The existing erasure obligation (F20) — cancelling the candidate's active booking and removing its calendar events — continues to apply; F23 adds the confirm-credential invalidation and cascade halt. This erasure-side work MUST run on the **asynchronous erasure-completion path** and MUST NOT block the erasure acknowledgement (the F04 `202 Accepted` within 2 s), inheriting the established "cleanup-incomplete" honest-bound on a provider failure rather than blocking erasure. After erasure, no usable confirm link and no calendar event survive for the erased data subject.

### Key Entities *(include if feature involves data)*

- **Booking (Confirmed Interview)** *(extended from F13/F20)*: gains a confirmation-attendance lifecycle layered over its existing scheduled/rescheduled/cancelled state — confirmation-requested (with timestamp), candidate-confirmed (with timestamp), confirmation-not-requestable (not contactable), unconfirmed-escalated (with timestamp), and released-as-unconfirmed. The unit whose state the cascade and the recruiter release mutate; slot release reuses the F20 cancellation/slot-release primitive with a distinct "released as unconfirmed / no-show" reason for analytics.
- **Confirmation Credential**: a candidate-bound, hashed, single-use, expiring token granting access to one booking's confirm action; reuses the established candidate-link token model (secure-token generator + HMAC hasher); invalidated on confirmation, release, cancel, reschedule, or ineligibility.
- **Cascade Schedule / Checkpoint** *(reuses F00.2)*: the `@Scheduled` task driving confirmation-request dispatch and unconfirmed escalation, governed by the shared scheduler-checkpoint for idempotency and missed-fire replay, over a covering index on the booking's start instant and cascade state.
- **Recruiter Notification** *(reused/extended from F22/F20)*: the value-free in-app/email signal raised when an interview is unconfirmed at the escalation deadline (the one-tap release prompt). A single coarse "interview unconfirmed" kind covers both candidate-non-response and could-not-auto-request (not contactable) so the recruiter signal is never a contactability oracle; append-only kind, referencing booking/candidate by internal identifier only, never carrying candidate-resolvable values.
- **Workspace No-Show Settings** *(extends F03 workspace config)*: the per-workspace confirmation-request lead time and unconfirmed-escalation deadline, with documented defaults and a validity constraint (escalation deadline not before the request lead time).
- **Audit Entry** *(reused/extended)*: append-only record of each confirmation, escalation, and release, keyed by candidate (internal identifier only), capturing actor, action, and timestamp — free of personal data and token values.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: For 100% of live confirmed bookings whose start crosses the configured lead-time boundary, exactly one consent-gated attendance-confirmation reminder is dispatched to the candidate (no duplicates across overlapping sweeps, restarts, or missed-fire replays), verified with a mocked/test clock.
- **SC-002**: A candidate can confirm attendance (open link → confirm) in under 1 minute with no login, on a page that passes WCAG 2.2 AA with zero axe violations, loads in under 2 seconds on a 4G simulation, and shows the interview time in the candidate's local time zone with DST-correct labelling.
- **SC-003**: For 100% of bookings still unconfirmed at the configured escalation deadline, exactly one recruiter "interview unconfirmed" alert is raised (idempotent across sweeps and a simulated mid-task restart), and zero alerts are raised for bookings the candidate confirmed or that were cancelled/rescheduled-away.
- **SC-004**: A recruiter one-tap release results, in 100% of cases, in the calendar events being removed for all participants, the slot released and immediately selectable again by the next computation, and the release audit-logged with the recruiter as initiator — verified against the test provider harness.
- **SC-005**: The required end-to-end no-show cascade passes as a single automated flow (test-clock injection): scheduled task fires → confirmation email sent → candidate remains unconfirmed → the booking reaches a **recorded** "unconfirmed — escalated" state → recruiter alert raised → recruiter releases slot → slot is available again in the database and the release is audit-logged. The intermediate escalated state MUST be observable in the booking record (not merely a transient signal).
- **SC-006**: A simulated mid-task restart (process kill during a cascade run) followed by restart does NOT send a duplicate confirmation email or raise a duplicate escalation for any booking, verified by integration test (the F00.2 idempotency contract).
- **SC-007**: Under genuinely simultaneous conflicting actions on one booking (two recruiter releases, or a release racing a candidate confirmation or an F20 cancel/reschedule) — exercised by a gated/latched concurrent test across multiple trials — exactly one authoritative transition commits and all others are cleanly refused, 100% of the time, with no double cancellation and no duplicate notifications.
- **SC-008**: An expired confirm link produces a distinct "expired" (410) experience in 100% of expired-link accesses, while used/invalid/unknown credentials are indistinguishable from one another (no existence oracle), the confirm endpoints are rate-limited (429 on breach), and a link-scanner GET never records a confirmation.
- **SC-009**: No confirmation reminder is dispatched for a candidate who fails the contactability check (consent missing/withdrawn/erased/over-retention/undeliverable) in 100% of such cases, and such bookings still escalate to the recruiter at the deadline; erasure of a candidate halts the cascade and invalidates the confirm credential with no usable link surviving.
- **SC-010**: Every confirmation, escalation, and release is represented by exactly one append-only audit entry recording actor, action, booking/candidate (internal id only), and timestamp, free of personal data and token values; zero occurrences of candidate personal data or token values appear in application logs across the full flow (verified by log scan).
- **SC-011**: Workspace-configured lead-time and escalation-deadline values change the cascade timing accordingly (verified with the test clock at non-default offsets), a contradictory configuration (escalation before request lead time) is rejected, and the documented defaults apply with zero admin action.
- **SC-012**: No SMS/WhatsApp dispatch path, dependency, configuration, or code reference exists in the MVP codebase, and no automatic waitlisted-candidate invitation path or waitlist data model exists — verified by source inspection.
- **SC-013**: A cascade whose lead-time or escalation boundary falls within one hour of a daylight-saving transition fires at the correct wall-clock moment (the offset computed against the interview's absolute start instant never drifts by an hour), verified by an integration test with a synthetic DST-crossing fixture and the test clock.

## Assumptions

- **Dependencies are complete and reused**: F13 single-stage scheduling (the booking aggregate, atomic reservation, candidate-link token model, audit trail), F20 reschedule/cancellation (the cancellation/slot-release primitive, the `CANCELLED`/`RESCHEDULED` states, the recruiter-notification value-free kinds, and the recovery-sweep pattern), F10/F11 calendar adapters (event removal, rollback, cleanup-incomplete handling), F12 rule engine (so a released slot recomputes as free), F21/F22 email (the `REMINDER_24H`/`REMINDER_1H` templates already exist; consent-gated candidate dispatch; the recruiter-notification path), F04 GDPR primitives (contactability gate, shared erasure wipe, candidate audit taxonomy), F03 workspace config (the new per-workspace cascade settings extend it), and F00.2 scheduler-checkpoint pattern. F23 orchestrates and extends these rather than reimplementing them.
- **Email-only cascade** (backlog scope): the confirmation request and the recruiter alert ride the existing email/notification channels. The SMS/WhatsApp nudge step of product-spec §4 Flow A4 is **deferred to v1.5** and absent from the MVP codebase (asserted by source inspection, FR-023/SC-012).
- **Waitlist auto-invitation is out of scope**: the F23 backlog acceptance criteria define one-tap slot **release**, not automatic waitlisted-candidate invitation. The released slot returns to general availability for normal scheduling/reschedule; automatic waitlist invitation is deferred. The product-spec FR-6 phrase "invite waitlisted candidate" is satisfied for the MVP by making the slot available again.
- **No-show analytics are F50**: F23 records the no-show signal and the confirmed/unconfirmed/released distinction; the dashboard "no-show rate" widget is owned by F50 (Core Dashboard). F23 must produce sufficient data and must not preclude that rendering, but does not build the dashboard.
- **No-show proxy definition**: Cadence cannot observe physical interview attendance (no attendance-tracking integration in the MVP). The no-show signal is therefore "interview start reached without a candidate confirmation" (and/or recruiter-released as unconfirmed). This proxy is defined explicitly so the F50 metric is unambiguous.
- **Confirmation-request lead-time default is 24 hours before start**, configurable per workspace (backlog US-F23-1/US-F23-4 and the F14 "24 h before" cascade reference).
- **Unconfirmed-escalation deadline default is 2 hours before start**, configurable per workspace (backlog US-F23-4). The escalation deadline MUST be strictly between the confirmation-request lead time and the interview start (request → escalation → start); this pinned default makes FR-015/SC-011 concretely verifiable. The backlog US-F23-1/US-F23-4 single "confirmation window" is intentionally decomposed into these two knobs (request lead time + escalation deadline).
- **Release notifies the candidate (reuses F20 recruiter-cancel)**: a recruiter one-tap release reuses the existing F20 recruiter-initiated cancellation primitive, which notifies the candidate of the cancellation over the consent-gated channel (consistent with F20 FR-013). F23 does not add a separate no-notify release path (§I YAGNI); the release is tagged with a distinct "released as unconfirmed / no-show" reason for the FR-016 analytics signal. The recruiter can alternatively initiate an F20 reschedule instead of releasing.
- **Candidate status page is F30**: rendering the confirmed / unconfirmed / released state on the candidate's own status page is F30's responsibility. F23 mutates the booking state F30 reads and must not preclude it; the post-confirmation acknowledgement directs the candidate appropriately rather than dead-ending.
- **Single-stage only**: the cascade applies to single-stage Flow A1 bookings. Multi-stage "loop" no-show handling (Flow A2) is out of scope and deferred to v1.5.
- **Confirmation credential delivery and reuse**: the confirm credential is delivered in the confirmation-request reminder email, bound to the booking, single-use for the positive confirmation, and reuses the existing 256-bit secure-token generator and HMAC token-hasher (well above the 128-bit floor) and the candidate-link 410/400/429 policy — not a new token mechanism. It is distinct from the F20 reschedule/cancel credential (a candidate may both confirm via this link and reschedule/cancel via the F20 link).
- **Candidate-facing UX standards** for the confirm page reuse the F14 candidate-scheduling-page standards (WCAG 2.2 AA via axe, mobile-first, < 2 s on 4G, Lighthouse performance ≥ 85, `$localize` for all strings). This feature must meet that bar on its new candidate surface.
- **Recruiter surfaces**: the unconfirmed alert and the one-tap release are surfaced on the existing per-candidate recruiter view (the F20 booking-status surface). The multi-candidate pipeline board and bulk release are F51; the dashboard no-show metric is F50.
- **MongoDB indexes & schema deltas** (to be declared/finalized in `plan.md`/`data-model.md` per the F00.1/F00.2 pattern — no scheduled task queries an uncovered collection): the booking's interview start instant currently lives inside the offered-slot snapshot and is **not** a queryable top-level field, so the cascade requires a **denormalized top-level booked-start instant** on the booking, set at every booked-commit site (initial book + F20 forward-commit/reschedule rounds), plus a **covering index over `{status, bookedStartAt, <cascade-stage>}`** for the sweep predicate (expressed as an explicit bounded `@Query`, not a derived multi-criteria finder — the F12 `InvalidMongoDbApiUsageException` lesson). The cascade adds **per-booking stage fields** (e.g. confirmation-requested-at, candidate-confirmed-at, escalated-at, no-show-at), advanced by per-booking CAS, **not** new `SchedulingStatus` enum values (mirroring how F20 added `cancelledAt`/`rescheduleInvitedAt` as fields). It also needs a **confirm-credential lookup index** (a partial-unique hashed-token field distinct from the F13 `tokenHash` and F20 `manageTokenHash`, carrying `write=NON_NULL` to avoid the F01 present-as-null collision), append-only additions to the value-free `RecruiterNotificationType` (a single coarse "interview unconfirmed" kind) and `AuthEventType` (confirmation / escalation / release — never reorder), and a per-workspace cascade-settings extension to the F03 workspace config; the new Mongock changeset is the next order off the highest applied (currently `"013"`). **No new infrastructure service, broker, or queue is introduced** (constitution C2), and **no dependency outside the fixed stack** is added (C4) — the cascade reuses `@Scheduled` + `SchedulerCheckpoint`, not a scheduling library or message broker. New enums are logged only via `.name()` (the logstash `kv` footgun).
- **Scheduler idempotency & missed-fire**: the cascade strictly follows the F00.2 `SchedulerCheckpoint` contract (RUNNING/COMPLETED checkpoints, missed-fire replay on startup older than the configured threshold), with each candidate dispatch and each escalation guarded by a per-booking-per-stage idempotency key, so correctness rests on the per-booking guard rather than single-threaded execution.
