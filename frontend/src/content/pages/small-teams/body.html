The gap in the market

Interview-scheduling software has consolidated around two poles. At one end sit general-purpose booking links — fine for a first screen, blind to everything after it. At the other sit enterprise coordination platforms — excellent if you employ recruiting coordinators, priced and designed on the assumption that you do. The recruiting team of one to five people, hiring a handful of roles at a time, is served by neither: too much process for a bare booking link, no coordinator to feed a coordination console.

Cadence is built for exactly that team. Not a cut-down enterprise product — a different shape of product, designed around one constraint: nobody at your company owns scheduling, so the tool has to.

What a small team actually needs

  • Self-scheduling that handles the real cases: panels with quorums, working hours, buffers between interviews, daily caps per interviewer — enforced in the slot math before a time is ever offered. One link also covers reschedules and cancellations.
  • Follow-through without a human: confirmations and reminders on schedule, a no-show confirmation cascade that asks the candidate to confirm ahead of time and alerts you only when something needs a decision.
  • Candidates who are not left guessing: a private, no-login status page per candidate — stage, next step, expected date — plus a nudge to you when anyone has waited too long. Small teams win offers on candidate experience; this is how it scales past your inbox.
  • The ATS kept honest: candidate sync from Greenhouse or Lever and scheduling outcomes written back automatically — or CSV import if your ATS is a spreadsheet.
  • GDPR without a compliance department: encryption at rest, contact consent recorded and enforced at send time, automated retention, one-click erasure. A five-person team has the same legal duties as a five-thousand-person one — Cadence is built for GDPR so those duties are operational, not aspirational.
  • Pricing you do not need procurement for: free during early access, transparent plans after. No demo call required to learn the price.

What we deliberately left out

Honesty about scope: Cadence does not do high-volume or campus hiring, interviewer-training programs, coordination queues, or load balancing across interviewer pools. Those are real needs — at a scale where you will have coordinators, and where an enterprise platform (see our honest comparisons with GoodTime and ModernLoop) is the right buy. A tool for small teams earns its simplicity by not pretending to be everything.
