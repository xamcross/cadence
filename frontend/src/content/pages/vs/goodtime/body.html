Two tools, two team sizes

GoodTime is one of the most established names in interview scheduling: an enterprise platform built around AI-assisted coordination, with broad ATS coverage, interviewer training and interview-intelligence features, and solutions for high-volume and campus hiring. Companies with large talent-acquisition organizations and dedicated coordinators choose it for good reasons, and this page will not pretend otherwise.

Cadence approaches the same core job — getting candidates and interviewers into the right room with the least friction — from the opposite end of the market: the recruiting team of one to a handful of people, usually in Europe, with no coordination function and no compliance department. The honest comparison is less "which feature list is longer" and more "which tool is shaped like your team".

Where the differences show up

  • Team model: enterprise platforms assume coordinators who manage scheduling as a job. Cadence assumes nobody owns scheduling, so the follow-through — confirmations, reminders, the no-show confirmation cascade, scorecard collection, ATS write-back — runs without a human driving it.
  • Candidate communication: Cadence gives every candidate a private, no-login status page with their stage, next step, and an expected date, and nudges the recruiter when a candidate has waited too long — the between-interviews experience, not just the booking.
  • Data protection: Cadence is built for GDPR: candidate data encrypted at rest, contact consent recorded and enforced at send time, automated retention, and one-click erasure that reaches scheduling artifacts and synced ATS fields. For an EU team, this is the difference between compliance work and a checkbox.
  • Integrations: GoodTime covers many ATS systems, which matters at enterprise scale. Cadence integrates deliberately with Greenhouse and Lever — plus Google Calendar and Microsoft 365, with CSV import for everyone else.
  • Buying: GoodTime is sold the enterprise way — talk to sales, annual contract. Cadence has transparent pricing and is free during early access; you can be scheduling the same afternoon.

Where GoodTime is the right answer

If you run hundreds of interviews a month, coordinate across many ATS and HRIS systems, staff a coordination team, or need interviewer-training and interview-intelligence tooling, an enterprise platform is built for exactly that — and GoodTime is a leader there. Cadence does not chase high-volume or campus hiring, and a team that needs those workflows should not force a small-team tool to do them.

Making the call

Count your interviews per month and the people who touch scheduling.
