Same problem, different center of gravity

ModernLoop is a well-regarded interview-scheduling platform for mid-market and enterprise talent teams: AI-assisted slot finding, scheduling queues, interviewer load balancing and training tracks, and reporting built for recruiting operations. Its center of gravity is the coordination function — making scheduling-as-a-job dramatically more efficient.

Cadence starts from a different premise: in most small companies, scheduling is not anyone's job. A recruiter or founder sends the link between two other tasks, and everything that follows — reminders, no-show recovery, status updates, scorecard chasing, ATS hygiene, data-protection duties — either happens automatically or does not happen. Cadence is built so it happens automatically.

Where the differences show up

  • After the booking: Cadence treats the booking as the start. The no-show confirmation cascade asks the candidate to confirm ahead of the interview, alerts the recruiter on silence, records the no-show, releases interviewer time, and offers a rebook — the playbook from our no-show guide, running unattended.
  • The waiting candidate: every candidate gets a private, no-login status page with stage, next step, and an expected date, plus recruiter nudges when anyone waits too long. Coordination platforms optimize the process's inside; Cadence also tends its outside.
  • Data protection: Cadence is built for GDPR — encryption at rest, consent recorded and enforced at send time, automated retention, one-click erasure reaching scheduling artifacts and synced ATS fields. For an EU team this is core product, not an enterprise add-on conversation.
  • Scale features vs simplicity: ModernLoop's queues, load balancing, and training tracks earn their complexity at coordination scale. Cadence deliberately ships the small-team subset — self-scheduling, panels, buffers, daily caps, ATS write-back — without the operational surface a small team would never staff.
  • Buying: Cadence is free during early access with transparent pricing to follow; you can try it against your real calendar today.

Where ModernLoop is the right answer

If you employ recruiting coordinators, schedule at a volume where load balancing across interviewer pools is a daily concern, or need operations-grade scheduling analytics, a coordination platform is the right shape — and ModernLoop is a strong one. Cadence does not try to be a coordination console for a ten-person TA org.

Making the call

The practical question is whether scheduling at your company is a function or a chore. If it is a function, evaluate ModernLoop and its peers. If it is a chore you want off your desk — with candidates kept informed and GDPR handled by design — see what Cadence does for small recruiting teams and start from the home page.
