Two plans, one honest split

Cadence has a Free plan and a Team plan, billed per workspace - never per candidate. Candidates never need an account and are never a billable seat. Checkout, invoices, VAT, and cancellation are handled by Freemius, our merchant of record; Cadence never sees or stores card details.

Free plan

Everything a small pipeline needs to schedule without chaos, free for as long as you like:

  • Candidate self-scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellation links.
  • Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 sync.
  • Private candidate status pages and consent-gated email with templates.
  • Standalone CSV import, interviewer scorecards, pipeline views, and dashboards.
  • GDPR tooling: encryption at rest, retention automation, one-click erasure - compliance is never paywalled.

Team plan

For teams running a serious hiring operation, Team adds the automation layer:

  • Greenhouse and Lever ATS sync with scheduling write-back.
  • No-show defense: confirmation cascades and recruiter alerts before interviews slip.
  • SLA nudges that catch silent candidates before the pipeline stalls.

Upgrading takes one checkout from workspace settings. Downgrading never deletes anything: connections and settings are kept, paused, and resume the moment you upgrade again.

Early-access workspaces

Workspaces from the early-access program moved to the Free plan when billing launched, with nothing deleted and nothing switched on silently. Upgrading to Team is a single checkout from workspace settings whenever the automation layer earns its keep for your team.
