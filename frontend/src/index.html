Cadence — interview scheduling that respects candidates

Cadence helps recruiters schedule interviews, prevent no-shows, and keep candidates informed — with no candidate account required. It syncs with Google and Microsoft calendars and is GDPR-safe by design.

What Cadence does

  • One-link self-scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellation.
  • A no-show confirmation cascade with recruiter alerts.
  • A private candidate status page with live stage, next step, and dates.
  • Consent-recorded email and one-click right-to-erasure.
